The Problem

Cloud AI Has a Trust Problem

✗

Your data leaves your network

Every query to a cloud AI service sends your proprietary designs, process data, and trade secrets through someone else's servers.

✗

Costs scale unpredictably

$500/month becomes $5,000 as your team adopts AI. Per-query pricing punishes success.

✗

Vendor lock-in

API changes, price hikes, or service shutdowns can halt your workflow overnight. You don't control the infrastructure.

✗

Compliance barriers

ITAR, FERPA, HIPAA, or internal IP policies may prohibit cloud AI entirely. Many companies simply go without.

The Solution

Everything Runs Inside Your Walls

⚖

Air-Gapped Architecture

Development labs are physically isolated from the internet. A firewall separates the secure access portal from sensitive workloads. No data path exists to the outside.

⚙

GPU-Accelerated Training

Dedicated graphics processors for AI model training and real-time inference. Fine-tune models on your actual data in hours, not weeks.

🛠

Isolated Virtual Environments

One server runs multiple isolated labs — AI training, firmware dev, CAD, data analysis, testing. Teams work independently without interference.

🌐

Secure Remote Access

Browser-based portal lets your team work from anywhere. The portal is firewalled — it can reach the labs, but the labs can't reach the internet.
