The Enterprise Review

Before a big customer signs, they check your security.

Enterprise procurement and SOC 2 auditors expect more than a scan — they expect an independent test, run by someone who isn't you. Here's the surface that review tends to look at first, for any company running a real product:

🌐

The web app

Authentication, session handling, access controls, and the everyday flows your customers touch — the most-probed surface in any review.

🔌

The API

The endpoints behind the app, where authorization gaps and data-exposure issues most often hide from a casual scan.

🔑

Identity & access

How accounts, roles, and tokens are issued and revoked — the controls an auditor asks you to evidence.

📊

The independent test

For SOC 2 Type II, an external penetration test every audit cycle — a predictable yearly need most teams scramble for at the last minute.
